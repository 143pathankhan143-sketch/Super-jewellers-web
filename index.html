HALLMARK & AUTHENTICITY

Purity,
certified.

Every gold piece we sell carries the BIS hallmark for 22K (916) and 24K purity — with the HUID you can check yourself on the BIS Care app. Our 925 sterling silver is stamped for purity.

Authenticity certificates for gold, diamonds and stones are provided on the actual pieces that carry them — ask in store for the hallmark and certificate of the piece you love. We never show certificates that do not belong to a real product.
