OUR STORY

Jewellery for
every milestone.

Super Jewellers is a physical jewellery showroom serving families across Latur for weddings, investments and special moments. Our floor brings together certified gold, sparkling diamond, pure 925 silver and special-occasion collections — all viewable in person, with expert guidance and zero pressure.

We believe buying jewellery should feel personal and transparent: you see the piece, you understand the pricing, and you carry home something you will treasure for years.

COLLECTIONS

Gold · Diamond · Silver · Special

22K (916) BIS hallmarked necklaces, rani haar, Arabic haar, minilacha, bangles, chains, earrings, rings and pendants — plus 925 silver (ZEON range), diamond jewellery, antique stone, Kuwaiti, Arbi, rose gold, pearl, 1-gram gold and Korean designs.

TRADITION

Traditional & modern designs

Traditional Maharashtrian craftsmanship sits alongside contemporary silhouettes — so every generation in the family finds something that feels like theirs.

22K BIS HALLMARKED

Certified purity, always

Gold jewellery carries the BIS hallmark for 22K (916) and 24K purity with HUID you can verify. 925 sterling silver is stamped for purity. Authenticity certificates are provided on the actual pieces that carry them.

CUSTOM ORDERS

Made for you

Custom jewellery orders are a core part of the house — share your design or redesign family gold with our craftsmen in gold, diamond and 925 silver.
