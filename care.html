KEEP IT SHINING

Jewellery care.

Simple habits that keep gold, diamond and 925 silver pieces beautiful for decades.

STORAGE

Store separately, softly

Keep each piece in its own soft pouch or lined box. Diamonds can scratch gold and silver, so never toss pieces together in one drawer.

EVERYDAY

Last on, first off

Put jewellery on after perfumes, lotions and hairspray, and take it off before swimming, exercising or housework. Moisture and chemicals dull the shine.

CLEANING

Gentle cleaning only

Wipe with a soft, dry, lint-free cloth after wear. For a deeper clean, use lukewarm water with a drop of mild soap, rinse, and pat dry — never harsh chemicals or abrasive powders.

SILVER

Silver needs air care

925 silver naturally tarnishes over time. Store it in airtight pouches with an anti-tarnish strip, and polish gently with a silver cloth when needed.

DIAMONDS

Check the settings

Once a year, have prongs and settings checked in-store. A quick inspection keeps stones secure and catches loose settings early.
