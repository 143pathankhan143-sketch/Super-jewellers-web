GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers about purity, reservations, appointments and custom orders at Super Jewellers, Latur.

PURITY

Is the gold BIS hallmarked?

Yes. Our gold jewellery is 22K (916) and 24K BIS hallmarked with HUID you can verify. 925 sterling silver is stamped for purity. Authenticity certificates are provided on the actual pieces that carry them.

RESERVATIONS

Does reserving online buy the piece?

No. Reserving on this website is only a request to view a piece in the showroom. No payment is taken online, and the piece is held only after the store approves your reservation. See the Reservation Policy.

APPOINTMENTS

How do I book a visit?

Use the Book a Visit page to pick a date and time. The store confirms your slot on WhatsApp. Walk-ins are welcome too — Monday – Saturday: 11:00 AM – 8:30 PM · Sunday: 11:00 AM – 6:00 PM.

CUSTOM ORDERS

Can I order custom jewellery?

Yes — custom orders are a core part of the house. Share your design or redesign family gold via the Custom Jewellery page. The store discusses design, material, budget and timeline with you before work begins.

PRICING

How is the price calculated?

Gold prices follow the daily market rate plus making charges and applicable taxes, shown transparently in-store. Prices on this website, where shown, are indicative — the final price is confirmed in the showroom at the time of purchase.

PAYMENTS

Which payments do you accept?

Cash, UPI and Net Banking. EMI options are available on eligible purchases — ask in-store for details.

CANCELLATION

How do I cancel a reservation?

Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 97644 53486 with your reference ID. See the Cancellation Policy for details.

CARE

How do I care for my jewellery?

Store pieces separately in soft pouches, keep them away from moisture and perfumes, and clean gently with a soft dry cloth. More detail on the Jewellery Care page.
