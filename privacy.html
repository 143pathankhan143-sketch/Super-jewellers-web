Privacy Policy

This page explains the website features and third-party links that may process information when you visit or contact Super Jewellers.

What this website does

The storefront displays jewellery collection information. The public site does not provide public account registration or online checkout. Product catalogue data and uploaded product photographs are served using Supabase. The separate admin page uses Supabase Authentication and is intended only for authorised staff.

When you contact us

This website has no public contact form. If you choose to contact Super Jewellers by WhatsApp, telephone, social media, or an external map/review link, the relevant provider may process information under its own privacy policy. Please avoid sending payment card details or other sensitive information through social messaging.

Service providers

Supabase provides database, image storage, and admin authentication services. Links may also open WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Maps, or Google review services. Their own privacy terms apply when you visit those services. Confirm the project settings and service retention policies before publishing.

Questions and requests

For a privacy question, contact the business using the verified details on the Contact section. Before publication, replace any contact placeholders with a monitored business contact.

This is a website-specific starter notice, not legal advice. Review it against the business’s actual data practices and applicable requirements before publishing.
