Privacy Policy

This page explains the website features and third-party links that may process information when you visit or contact Super Jewellers.

What this website does

This is a static information website for Super Jewellers, Latur. It shows the collections, showroom address, opening hours and contact details. The site has no user accounts, no login, no contact forms and no online checkout, and it does not store any visitor information.

When you contact us

This website has no public contact form. If you choose to contact Super Jewellers by WhatsApp, telephone, social media, or an external map/review link, the relevant provider may process information under its own privacy policy. Please avoid sending payment card details or other sensitive information through social messaging.

Service providers

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs for security and delivery. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Buttons and links may open WhatsApp, Instagram or Google Maps, and their own privacy terms apply when you use those services.

Questions and requests

For any privacy question, please contact the business using the details in the Contact section.
