Reservation Policy

A reservation on this website is a request to view a piece in the showroom — never an online purchase. This policy explains how it works.

1. What a reservation means

Submitting the reservation form sends your name, mobile number, chosen pieces and preferred visit date and time to the store. No payment is taken online and no piece is sold or shipped through this website.

2. Approval and confirmation

Every reservation starts as pending. The store checks availability and confirms or declines your request on WhatsApp or by phone. A piece is held for you only after the store approves your reservation — until then, it remains available to other customers.

3. Holding period

Approved pieces are kept aside for your scheduled visit. If you need a different date or time, contact the store with your reference ID and we will adjust where possible. Pieces not collected or re-scheduled within the agreed window return to general availability.

4. Pricing

Gold and silver prices follow the daily market rate at the time of purchase, plus making charges and applicable taxes. Any indicative price shown on this website is confirmed in-store before you buy.

5. Your visit

Please carry a valid ID and your reference ID when you visit. Showroom hours: Monday – Saturday: 11:00 AM – 8:30 PM · Sunday: 11:00 AM – 6:00 PM.

6. Cancellations

You can cancel or change a reservation anytime before your visit — see the Cancellation Policy.
