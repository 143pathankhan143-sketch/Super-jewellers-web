Cancellation Policy

Plans change — you can cancel or reschedule a reservation or appointment at no charge. Here is how.

1. How to cancel

Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 97644 53486 with your reference ID (for example, SJ-R-…). The store will mark your reservation or appointment as cancelled.

2. Rescheduling

Prefer a different date or time? Tell us your new preference with your reference ID and we will re-confirm your slot and any held pieces, subject to availability.

3. Held pieces

When a reservation is cancelled, any pieces held for you return to general availability. If you cancel after the agreed holding window, the same applies.

4. No charges

There is no cancellation fee for reservations or appointments made through this website, since no payment is taken online. Purchases completed in-store follow the terms shared at the time of sale.

5. Custom orders

Custom jewellery orders are made to your design. Once work has begun, cancellation terms are discussed with you in-store before production starts — please ask us directly for custom-order changes.
